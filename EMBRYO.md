# Embryo Docs Online

Embryo Docs Online is Embryo-Zero's fork of [DocumentServer](https://github.com/ONLYOFFICE/DocumentServer) (9d8ece1, commit 9d8ece1). It does office editing in the browser.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
